# FocusForge Design Bible

Version: 1.0

---

# Our Mission

Build beautiful physical products that help people focus without adding more distractions.

Technology should disappear into the background.

The user should focus on their work—not on the device.

---

# Design Principles

1. One button.

2. No screen.

3. Calm technology.

4. Premium materials.

5. Beautiful animations.

6. Easy to understand.

7. Built to last.

---

# Product Philosophy

FocusForge should feel more like a piece of furniture than a gadget.

It should look good even when it is turned off.

---

# User Experience

The user should be able to understand the product without reading a manual.

Every interaction should feel intentional.

Nothing should be loud.

Nothing should flash aggressively.

Everything should feel calm.
